# Bridges: in-game tests and research

Notes for the v2.1.0 Bridge tool. The bridges here are the ones the Highways stage builds (`Generate/HighwayStage.cs`); the tool will place the same pieces.

## Bridge tool ramps and drawn roads

Since v2.4.0, taking down a newly built bridge leaves later ground and road edits in place (#306). When Generate rebuilds the bridge, each ramp keeps the grade of existing road cells beyond its end. Bridges from older versions use their original ground records until they are rebuilt.

## Generated bridges in game
- **Tested in game 2026-11-12** (world from `mapeditor-cli new 6144 --seed 3 --shape archipelago --islands 5 --land 40 --roughness 60 --biomes centerforest --generate --bridges`, 4 bridges, game V 3.4.0):
  - **Good:** the end pieces face the right way (caps toward the land) on north-south and east-west bridges; the 18 m highway narrowing onto the 14 m deck is fine; a houseboat fits under; fast vehicles and a bicycle cross on and off the ends without catching.
  - **A seam where the ground meets the bridge:** a V-groove along the deck's sides and across each end, and a square pit around every pillar. Cause (from `Prefab.CopyIntoLocal`): a solid non-terrain block copied from a prefab (the deck's `bridgeConcreteBlock`, the pillars) gets air density, so the terrain surface curves down into it. POIs hide this with terrain filler blocks at their edges (`terrainFillerType`/`terrainFiller2Type`); the `rwgonly` bridge pieces have none (RWG never places them).
  - **Heights can't fix it** (tried in a test world, bridges 1 and 3 changed, 2 and 4 as before): ground at the ends raised from the deck's top block (37.0) to 37.45 put the ground above the deck, so it looked sunk, and the groove stayed; footing tops lowered from the pieces' bottom layer (25.00) to 24.45 left the pits as they were. Keep the heights as generated. (The game does copy a piece's bottom layer with its air, as layers below `YOffset` always are, but that wasn't the cause.)
  - **So the seam is cosmetic only** (tracked as [jayfitt-droid/Gnostic-7d2d-map-editor#5](https://github.com/jayfitt-droid/Gnostic-7d2d-map-editor/issues/5)), and the bridges are good to build the tool on. If it's ever worth hiding, try laying a small game prefab made of terrain blocks (an asphalt patch) across each deck end; the deck-wreck tests showed the game accepts small prefabs on the deck.
  - **Terrain-block patches don't hide it either** (tested in game 2026-11-13, the same seed-3 world, 5 bridges, 4 ends left as controls). The patch was `Parts/part_driveway_countrytown_asphalt_04`: a 4x1x2 slab of 8 `terrAsphalt` blocks, the only small all-terrain prefab a scan of every game prefab found (the others are a pothole, a crater, or carry a car). It was laid 3-4 across each 14-block deck end in three layouts: **A** straddling the joint (deck's last row + first ramp row) flush with the deck top; **B** two ramp rows flush with the deck top; **C** one block higher over the deck's last row and the first ramp row, inside the rail poles. The user found none hid the groove. Validate was unchanged by the patches (they're tagged `part`). So #5 stays a `known-issue`; the user manual says so. What's left untried needs blocks the bridge pieces don't have (`terrainFiller` in the pieces themselves), which would mean shipping a changed game prefab: not allowed.
  - **Validate and `navonly` bridges (built 2026-11-13):** the game's own bridges (`bridge_concrete1`, `departure_bridge_1_signs`, ...) carry the `navonly` tag, are placed by hand in Navezgane, lie over water and carry sign prefabs on top. Validate skips them for the under-water check, skips bridge-on-bridge overlaps, and skips any `navonly` prefab whose centre is on a bridge (so deck wrecks pass too). Ordinary POIs on a bridge are still flagged.
  - The game log's `[MultiBlockManager][Alignment] SetTerrainAlignmentDirty failed` warnings near the bridge approaches come from the game's own road wrecks (`carsRandomHelper` in `biomes.xml`, placed with `SetBlockRaw` so never registered), not from the editor.

## Car wrecks on bridge decks

The user's idea (2026-11-12), tested in game in three test worlds (the third all clear). **Built 2026-11-13 (step 5, not yet checked in game)** in `BridgeOp.Wrecks`: one per 100-150 m (the first half a gap in, never on the end sections), alternating lanes (car block on block 4 or 9) and every second wreck turned the other way, picked by weight (`player_start1`-`6` 4 each, ambulance and army truck 2 each), the army truck turned like the rest (the plan says check it in game). The ambulance and army truck are tagged `part`, not `navonly`; Validate skips `part` prefabs altogether, and the cars are skipped by the `navonly`-on-a-bridge rule. Seeded by the highway seed (stage 4) or by the bridge's axis, line, start and length (the Bridge tool), so a height change never reshuffles them. Options: Generate > Highways "Car wrecks on bridges" (`HighwayOptions.DeckWrecks`, saved, default on) and the Bridge tool's "Car wrecks" box (not saved). The game can't decorate decks itself: it adds road wrecks (`carsRandomHelper` and others, `biomes.xml` `blockontop`) on cells painted as road in `splat3.png`, on the ground there, and bridges paint no road under the deck (it would put the cars on the sea floor). The bridge pieces hold no vehicles. So the editor places wreck prefabs on the deck as `prefabs.xml` decorations, which the game accepts.
  - **Prefabs:** `POIs/player_start1`–`6`: a random car (`carsRandomHelper`) plus a little loot (backpack, cooler, trash, purse), one block tall, no ground blocks, no sleepers; `navonly` only keeps RWG from picking them. Maybe also `Parts/part_ambulance_01` and `Parts/part_armytruck_01` as rarer wrecks; they have sleeper volumes, so zombies may wake on the bridge (**the user to decide**). Not `Parts/part_car_accident_01`: its bottom layer is an asphalt patch (`terrAsphalt`) that lifts the cars a block and doesn't match the deck.
  - **Height:** the wreck's bottom layer one above the deck's top block: bridge piece y + `BridgeDeck` + 1.
  - **Turn like the bridge pieces:** 0 or 2 on a north-south bridge, 1 or 3 on an east-west one. The car blocks have rotation 0, so each car lies along its prefab's own z. An extra turn (tried to fit a wide prefab) put the car across the deck into the rails.
  - **Place by the car block, not the footprint:** the car is one block that spawns as a whole car centred on it, spilling past the prefab's footprint (in `player_start2` it is on the prefab's edge). Put the car block on a lane's middle, 4 or 9 blocks across the 14-block deck, and keep the whole footprint inside 1-12 (guard rail poles stand on 0 and 13 only). Car blocks at layer 0 (x, z): player_start1 (3,1), 2 (0,5), 3 (3,2), 4 (3,3), 5 (3,5), 6 (3,0); ambulance (2,4); army truck (3,6), its block turned 1 (check it lies along the deck).
  - **Suggested shape (to confirm):** the highway stage adds them while building each bridge, about one per 100-150 m, alternating lanes and directions, chosen by the highway seed; an option to turn them off; the bridge tool uses the same code. Validate must stop flagging a deck wreck as overlapping the bridge or as under water.
